\noindent Research basis:
\begin{itemize}
\item Agent evaluation research motivates fixed task environments, repeated trials, and cost-aware metrics.
\item Prompt-compression and long-context research motivate decision-preserving context control rather than shortest-prompt contests.
\item RAG and evidence-recall research motivate explicit recall floors for hidden or compressed evidence.
\item Reproducible-build and supply-chain practice motivates hash-bound research, package, release, and provenance artifacts.
\item TRICE treats its literature base as a versioned product input: if the ledger drifts, the paper, README, and integrity proof must be regenerated.
\end{itemize}
